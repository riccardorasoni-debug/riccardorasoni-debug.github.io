## Research question

Does repeated participation in a survey change the economic expectations that respondents report? And how does survey design shape these effects?

## Approach

This project compares survey tenure patterns in European and US household expectations panels, examining point forecasts, probabilistic beliefs, and measures of uncertainty. It investigates how the elicitation environment may affect responses over time.
